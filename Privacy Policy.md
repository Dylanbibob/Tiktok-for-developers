Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

### 1. Information We Collect

#### A. Information Provided via TikTok Login
When you choose to authenticate and log into Junstone using "Log in with TikTok", we may receive the following standard profile information authorized by you:
* **Open ID**: A unique, anonymized identifier assigned to you by TikTok.
* **Basic Profile Information**: Your public display name and profile picture avatar.

We do NOT request or store your TikTok password, private messages, or friend lists.

#### B. Usage and Activity Information
When you use our Service, we automatically collect certain usage data, including:
* Watch history and playback progress.
* Unlocked episodes, digital coin balances, and transaction history.
* Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device details, and operating system.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
* **Authentication**: To verify your identity and maintain your logged-in session.
* **Service Continuity**: To save and sync your drama watch progress, favorite playlists, and unlocked content across sessions.
* **Customer Support**: To investigate technical issues and assist with account-related inquiries.
* **Platform Security**: To detect, prevent, and address unauthorized activity or fraud.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion (User Rights)

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide you with the Service. 

#### How to Request Data Deletion:
In compliance with international data privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA):
1. **Via TikTok App**: You can revoke Junstone's access permissions at any time by going to your TikTok account **Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions** and removing Junstone.
2. **Direct Erasure Request**: You may request the permanent deletion of your Junstone account and associated data by emailing us at **dingyuliang95@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*. Upon receiving your request, we will verify your identity and delete your profile data from our databases within 30 days.
